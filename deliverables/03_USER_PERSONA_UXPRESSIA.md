## 3. Goals, Needs & Motivations

### Primary Goals
1. **Consolidated Net Overview**: View available funds across checking accounts and credit cards in a single visual dashboard without logging into multiple bank portals.
2. **Effortless Expense Tracking**: Record non-synced transactions or split expenses in under 5 seconds.
3. **Achieve Savings Milestones**: Save at least $800/month consistently toward an emergency fund and international travel budget.
4. **Smart Category Guardrails**: Keep dining out and entertainment under $500/month without feeling restricted by an overly rigid zero-based budget.

### Core Motivations
- **Financial Security**: Avoiding the emotional stress of living paycheck-to-paycheck despite earning a good salary.
- **Dopamine from Progress**: Feeling a sense of accomplishment when seeing visual budget bars stay green and savings balances grow.
- **Aesthetic Appreciation**: Using apps that feel as sleek, dark, and refined as modern consumer software (Linear, Apple Card, Craft).

## 4. Frustrations & Pain Points

| Pain Point | Impact on User Experience |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Spreadsheet Fatigue** | Tried Google Sheets 3 times; stopped after 3 weeks because manual formula maintenance felt like a second unpaid job. |
| **Information Overload in Traditional Apps** | Bank apps and Mint clones show endless lists of numbers, dense tables, and annoying credit card loan ads. |
| **Delayed Realization** | Realizing they spent $450 on weekend dining only when the credit card statement arrives 20 days later. |
| **Clunky Multi-Step Modals** | Having to select 8 dropdowns (Date, Payee, Category, Sub-category, Account, Note, Tax, Tag) just to log a $6 latte. |

### Pains (Fears & Obstacles)
- Running out of liquidity before month-end due to hidden recurring subscriptions.

### Gains (Desires & Success Metrics)

- Beautiful progress bars that turn amber/coral when nearing category thresholds.

## 6. User Journey / "A Day in the Life" with MyBudget

### Scenario: Logging a Lunch & Checking Spend Velocity
1. **08:30 AM — Morning Glance**: Alex opens MyBudget on their iPhone. The sleek dark dashboard displays their total Wallet balance (`$15,630.71`) and the Spending bar showing 28% of October budget consumed. No ads, no anxiety.
2. **12:45 PM — Lunch with Coworkers**: Alex pays $18.50 at a local bistro. Alex taps the prominent `+` button directly from the quick action grid, types `18.50`, taps "Food", and taps Save. Time taken: **3.8 seconds**.
3. **06:00 PM — Evening Insight Check**: Alex receives a gentle push notification: *"You've used 65% of your Dining budget with 14 days remaining."* Alex opens the Budgets tab, toggles Auto-Budgeting on, and reads a 30-second tip in "Tips & Training" on meal prep savings.
4. **Outcome**: Alex feels empowered, in control, and confident in their financial trajectory.
